---
title: Author with AI
description: Use the course authoring chat to propose or revise lessons.
---

Open a course, then click **Author with AI**. Studio opens `/teacher/courses/[id]/author`, a chat workspace for lesson planning.

1. Describe what you want: audience, week, project, tool, constraints, and assessment goals.
2. Review the AI-proposed lesson cards. Each card uses a structured lesson format.
3. Click **Create lesson** on any card you want to add to the course.
4. To revise existing lessons, be specific: “make lessons 4–7 coding” or “turn lesson 3 into an image-to-code bridge.”
5. Apply the revised cards when the media types, titles, and prompts look right.

> If something goes wrong: ask for fewer lessons and more constraints. “Give me three 45-minute p5.js lessons for beginners” works better than “make this better.”
