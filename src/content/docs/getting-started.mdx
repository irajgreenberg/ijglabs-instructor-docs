---
title: Getting started
description: Sign in and open the Teacher Dashboard.
---

Before you begin, you need a Studio account with the `teacher` or `admin` role. An admin assigns this role. If the Teacher Dashboard is missing, contact your admin.

1. Go to [ijglabs.ai/login](https://ijglabs.ai/login).
2. Sign in with Google OAuth, or enter your email to receive a magic link.
3. After login, find the sidebar on the left.
4. Click the **graduation-cap icon** to open `/teacher`, the Teacher Dashboard.
5. Confirm you can see your courses list and the **Create course** action.
