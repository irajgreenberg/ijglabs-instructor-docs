---
title: Cohort overview
description: Understand the one-cohort-per-course model.
---

Each course owns exactly one cohort. To run two sections, **clone the course** and give the clone a different Section value. Each clone gets a separate roster, pending list, invite history, and status.

Cohort statuses:

- `draft`: default while you build the course and roster.
- `active`: ready for students to join and work.
- `archived`: kept for records, not current instruction.

The roster has enrolled students, who have signed in and joined, plus pending students, who are whitelisted but have not signed in yet. Pending students promote automatically to enrolled on first sign-in, so you can import a registrar CSV before students have accounts.
