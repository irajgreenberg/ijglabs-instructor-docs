---
title: Add students
description: Add one email, import a CSV roster, or use admin whitelist.
---

Use the cohort panel on the course detail page.

1. **Single email**: click **Add student**. If the student already has an account, they join immediately. If not, their email is whitelisted with this cohort pre-assigned and they auto-join after first sign-in.
2. **CSV import**: click **Import CSV** and upload any registrar export with an email column. The parser detects headers such as “Email” or “Email address,” or scans the first 25 rows for values containing `@`. Name columns are optional and auto-detected. Preview shows rows in file, valid emails, skipped rows, and duplicates. After import, the result panel lists added, pre-assigned, already in cohort, and errors.
3. **Manual whitelist**: admins can go to `/admin`, open the Whitelist tab, pre-authorize an email, and pin it to this cohort.
