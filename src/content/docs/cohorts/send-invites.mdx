---
title: Send invites
description: Email pending students their sign-in path.
---

Invite emails go only to pending students: whitelisted cohort members who have not signed in yet.

1. Open the course detail page and find the cohort header.
2. Click **Send invites (N)**. `N` is the pending student count.
3. Studio sends a friendly HTML email to each pending student. The link opens `/login?email=their-email`, so the sign-in form is pre-filled.
4. Students click **Email me a sign-in link** to receive their Auth.js magic link.
5. Read the status banner at the top of the panel after the send finishes.

You can also send to one pending student with the per-row **Send** icon. Each recipient has a 60-second cooldown. If you click twice too quickly, Studio skips recently invited students and reports `X skipped (sent less than 60s ago)`. Pending rows show **Invited 12m ago** after contact; the per-row icon resends later.
