IJG Studio Instructor is the teacher workspace for building AI art courses, assigning lessons, importing student cohorts, and sending sign-in invites from one place. Follow these short guides in order and you can publish a course with a CSV roster in about 15 minutes.

## I want to…

- [Create my first course](/courses/create-with-wizard/) — use the four-step Course Wizard to turn a syllabus into editable lessons.
- [Add a cohort](/cohorts/add-students/) — add one student, import a CSV roster, or pre-authorize emails.
- [Publish and invite students](/publish/) — choose enrollment mode, publish, then send invite emails.
